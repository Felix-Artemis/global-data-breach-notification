# 美国各州 —— 受保护个人信息（完整图表）

来源：用户提供的 Practical Law《各州数据泄露法受保护个人信息图表：概览》`[PL-USPI]`。

各州共同基准：**名字或首字母 + 姓氏**，加上以下至少一项 —— 社会保障号码（SSN）／驾驶执照或州身份证号码／金融账户、信用卡或借记卡号码**并附**必需的安全码、访问码或密码。

每项条目涵盖：**替代标识符**（无需姓名即可成立者）· **附加数据要素**（与标准清单不同的变化）· **安全港**（加密、脱敏或以其他方式保护是否可免于通知）· **排除项**（被划出个人信息范围的信息）。

**依赖任何条目之前，请先对照所引成文法条文核实。** 成文法常被修正。本图表仅涵盖普遍适用的泄露通知成文法 —— 不含分业法、不含仅适用于州机关的条款，亦不含综合性隐私法（CCPA/CPRA、CPA、TDPSA 等），后者使用不同的个人信息定义。

## 阿拉巴马州

**引注：** Ala. Code § 8-38-2(6)

**替代定义** —— 无。

**附加数据要素** —  Tax identification number. Passport, military identification, or other unique government-issued identification number. Financial account number, including a bank account, credit card, or debit card number, in combination with any required security or access code, password, expiration date, or PIN that is necessary to access the financial account or to conduct a credit or debit transaction. Information about an individuals medical history, mental or physical condition, or an individuals medical treatment or diagnosis by a healthcare professional. Health insurance policy or subscriber identification number and any unique identifier a health insurer uses to identify the individual. A username or email address, in combination with a password or security question and answer that would permit access to an online account affiliated with the covered entity that is reasonably likely to contain or is used to obtain personal information.

**安全港** — Yes, if the information has been truncated, encrypted, secured, or otherwise made unusable, unless the covered entity knows or has reason to know that the encryption key or security credential has also been breached. Information that is lawfully made available to the general public from either: Federal, state, or local government records. Widely distributed media.
