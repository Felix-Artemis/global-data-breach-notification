# global-data-breach-notification
A global data breach notification assessment Skill covering jurisdictions outside Mainland China.

The Skill produces structured conclusions across four dimensions: (1) response and notification deadlines; (2) whether and how affected individuals must be notified, including applicable thresholds and mandatory notice content; (3) whether and how regulators must be notified, including applicable thresholds and required information; and (4) any other mandatory remedial or response measures.

Use this Skill for questions concerning breach notification obligations, reporting deadlines, incident-response timelines, regulatory notification thresholds, consumer notification requirements, multi-jurisdiction breach-response planning, or the preparation of breach-notification questionnaires and checklists.

Typical questions include: “How long do we have to report a breach in Jurisdiction X?”, “Do we need to notify affected customers?”, “What must a breach notice contain?”, and “Which regulator must be notified?”

Coverage includes the EU/EEA, UK, U.S. federal law and all 50 states plus the District of Columbia, Canada (federal, Québec and Alberta), Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, India, Malaysia, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay, Russia, Israel, the UAE (onshore, DIFC, ADGM and DHCC), Saudi Arabia, Switzerland, South Africa, Türkiye, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, Egypt, Kenya, Nigeria, and jurisdictions recognized by the EU as providing an adequate level of data protection.
